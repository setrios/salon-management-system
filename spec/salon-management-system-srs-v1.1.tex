\section{Introduction}

\subsection{Purpose}
This document specifies the baseline requirements for the Salon Management
System: an information system for client record-keeping and service booking
for a single-location unisex beauty salon. It supersedes the informal
concept note \texttt{DESCRIPTION.md} as the controlling requirements
artifact once approved at SPEC-GATE (Section~\ref{sec:gate}).

\subsection{Scope}
The system automates client booking, master schedule management, material
stock tracking (general accounting only), and basic financial reporting for
one salon location. It does not include a client account system, a loyalty
program, automatic payroll calculation, no-show tracking, recipe-based
material deduction, or an online payment gateway (Section~\ref{sec:constraints}).

\subsection{Actors}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2cm]
  \item \textbf{Client} --- books a service without registration, using
    name and phone number.
  \item \textbf{Master} --- performs services; manages their own schedule
    and sees their own appointment calendar; authenticates with login/password.
  \item \textbf{Administrator} --- manages reference data, appointments,
    materials, and reporting; single administrator account; authenticates
    with login/password.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Document Overview}
Section~\ref{sec:general} gives the general description. Section~\ref{sec:glossary}
is the domain glossary. Section~\ref{sec:functional} lists functional
requirements. Section~\ref{sec:nonfunctional} lists non-functional
requirements. Section~\ref{sec:bdd} gives BDD scenarios for key flows.
Section~\ref{sec:traceability} is the traceability matrix.
Section~\ref{sec:gate} records the SPEC-GATE decision.

\section{General Description}
\label{sec:general}

\subsection{Superseded Decisions}
\label{sec:superseded}
Two decisions confirmed in \texttt{DESCRIPTION.md} \S8 are explicitly
revised by this SRS, as a result of the SKED dialogue:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.2cm]
  \item \emph{``The only SMS notification sent is a reminder before the
    visit --- there is no SMS for booking confirmation or cancellation.''}
    \textbf{Revised:} the system now sends a booking confirmation SMS and a
    reschedule confirmation SMS (REQ-F-005, REQ-F-009). No additional SMS is
    sent specifically for cancellation --- the client sees the updated
    status on the confirmation page (REQ-F-004, REQ-F-006).
  \item \emph{``The master/administrator do not receive a notification when
    a new booking is made or cancelled.''}
    \textbf{Revised:} masters and administrators now receive notifications
    for new, rescheduled, and cancelled appointments, through independently
    configurable channels (REQ-F-016, REQ-F-023, REQ-F-026).
\end{enumerate}
All other decisions in \texttt{DESCRIPTION.md} \S8 remain in force and are
treated as constraints of this SRS (Section~\ref{sec:constraints}).

\subsection{Product Functions}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2cm]
  \item Client: multi-service booking, immediate confirmation, free
    cancellation and reschedule via a link, SMS notifications.
  \item Master: schedule view, own service/price list, availability
    management, notifications.
  \item Administrator: reference data management, appointment management,
    material tracking, payment recording, reporting, notifications.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Constraints}
\label{sec:constraints}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2cm]
  \item Single salon location, single time zone, no branch network.
  \item 30-minute appointment slot granularity.
  \item No client account/password; client identified by name and phone
    number only.
  \item No online payment gateway; only the fact of payment is recorded,
    after service completion.
  \item No automatic material deduction by service ``recipe''; materials
    tracked as general receipt/issue accounting only.
  \item No loyalty program, no automatic master payroll calculation, no
    client no-show tracking.
  \item No self-service password reset or account recovery for Master or
    Administrator accounts; credential resets are an operational matter
    outside this version's scope (added in v1.1, closing review finding A4).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Assumptions and Dependencies}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2cm]
  \item SMS sending is simulated (no real SMS gateway integration in this
    version), consistent with \texttt{DESCRIPTION.md}.
  \item Expected usage scale is not numerically fixed for this version; no
    specific throughput or concurrency target is set as an NFR.
\end{itemize}

\section{Domain Glossary}
\label{sec:glossary}

\begin{longtable}{L{3.4cm}L{10.6cm}}
\toprule
Term & Definition \\
\midrule
\endhead
Client & A person who books a service by providing name and phone number;
  has no account in the system. \\
Master & A staff member who performs services; authenticates with an
  individual login (username and password) created by the administrator;
  has own service/price list and own availability schedule. Self-service
  password reset is out of scope (see Constraints). \\
Administrator & The single staff account with full management access to
  reference data, appointments, materials, and reports; authenticates with
  an individual login (username and password). Self-service password reset
  is out of scope (see Constraints). \\
Service & A definition of a performable procedure: name, category, base
  duration. Categories are free-form, created by the administrator (see
  REQ-F-024); not a fixed enumeration. \\
MasterService & The link between a master and a service, carrying that
  master's individual price for that service. \\
Appointment & A single client visit: client, master, date/time, status,
  and an unguessable access token used for the confirmation page. Status is
  one of \texttt{confirmed} (set automatically on creation, REQ-F-003),
  \texttt{completed} (set by the administrator, REQ-F-027), or
  \texttt{cancelled} (set by the client, REQ-F-006). A reschedule
  (REQ-F-007, REQ-F-028) updates date/time and master on this same record
  and does not create a new Appointment. \\
AppointmentService & One of the services included in a single appointment;
  an appointment may include several. \\
Confirmation Page & The web page shown to the client immediately after
  booking and reachable again via the link sent by SMS; shows appointment
  details and the cancel/reschedule controls. The page and its access token
  remain valid after cancellation: the page then shows status
  \texttt{cancelled} and the cancel/reschedule controls are disabled
  rather than the link being invalidated. \\
Payment & A record of amount, status (paid/unpaid), and payment method for
  an appointment; created only after the service is completed. \\
Material & A stock item: name, unit of measure (from the standard list or a
  custom unit), current stock level. \\
MaterialTransaction & A recorded receipt or issue of a material: date,
  quantity. \\
Notification & An event-driven message (booking confirmation, reschedule
  confirmation, 24-hour reminder, 2-hour reminder to the client; new /
  reschedule / cancellation alert to master and administrator), each with a
  channel and a send status (sent / skipped / failed). \\
Reminder & The 24-hour or 2-hour client SMS before the appointment; skipped
  (not sent retroactively) if its scheduled time has already passed at the
  moment of booking or reschedule. \\
Notification Preference & A master's or administrator's own on/off setting
  for a given notification channel (SMS, in-app) and a given event type
  (new, reschedule, cancellation). \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\section{Functional Requirements}
\label{sec:functional}

Each requirement has a stable identifier \texttt{REQ-F-NNN}. Acceptance
criteria are stated as observable conditions.

\begin{longtable}{L{1.9cm}L{6.4cm}L{6.2cm}}
\toprule
ID & Requirement & Acceptance Criterion \\
\midrule
\endhead
REQ-F-001 & The client can select one or more services for a single visit;
  total duration and cost are the sum of the selected services. &
  Booking with 2+ services shows a single duration and a single cost equal
  to the sum of the individual services. \\
REQ-F-002 & The client selects a master, date, and time from that master's
  available 30-minute slots. & Only slots within the selected master's set
  availability, and not already booked, are selectable. \\
REQ-F-003 & The appointment is confirmed automatically on creation; no
  manual approval step exists. & Immediately after booking, the appointment
  status is ``confirmed'' with no pending/approval state observable. \\
REQ-F-004 & After a successful booking, the system shows a confirmation
  page with appointment details and cancel/reschedule links. & The page is
  displayed right after booking and is reachable again later via the SMS
  link (REQ-F-005). \\
REQ-F-005 & The system sends the client a booking confirmation SMS
  (simulated) with a link to the confirmation page. & Exactly one
  confirmation SMS per successful booking is recorded, containing a valid
  link to REQ-F-004's page. \\
REQ-F-006 & The client can cancel the appointment via the link, free of
  charge, with no restriction on notice period. & Cancelling at any time,
  including minutes before the appointment, succeeds without a fee or
  error. \\
REQ-F-007 & The client can reschedule the appointment via the link, free of
  charge, with no restriction on notice period or number of times. &
  Rescheduling repeatedly, including shortly before the appointment,
  succeeds each time without a fee or error. \\
REQ-F-008 & On reschedule, the 24-hour and 2-hour reminder times are
  recalculated from the new appointment time. & After a reschedule, the
  stored reminder times equal new\_time~$-$~24h and new\_time~$-$~2h. \\
REQ-F-009 & On reschedule, the system sends a new SMS with the updated
  appointment details and a link to the (same) confirmation page. & Exactly
  one reschedule SMS per successful reschedule is recorded. \\
REQ-F-010 & The system sends a 24-hour reminder SMS, unless that time has
  already passed at the moment of booking or reschedule, in which case it
  is recorded as skipped. & A booking made $<$24h before the appointment
  shows the 24h reminder with status ``skipped''; otherwise ``sent'' at the
  correct time. \\
REQ-F-011 & The system sends a 2-hour reminder SMS under the same rule as
  REQ-F-010. & A booking made $<$2h before the appointment shows the 2h
  reminder with status ``skipped''. \\
REQ-F-012 & A master authenticates with login and password; the account is
  created by the administrator. & A master cannot self-register; login
  succeeds only for an account created by the administrator. \\
REQ-F-013 & A master can view their own appointment calendar. & The
  calendar shown to a logged-in master contains only that master's
  appointments. \\
REQ-F-014 & A master can view the services they perform and their own
  prices. & The list shown matches exactly the MasterService records for
  that master. \\
REQ-F-015 & A master sets their availability using a recurring weekly
  pattern, specific dates, or both. & Both a weekly-repeating slot and a
  one-off date-specific slot can be created and take effect for booking. \\
REQ-F-016 & A master receives a notification (SMS and/or in-app, per their
  own preferences) when one of their own appointments is created,
  rescheduled, or cancelled. & Disabling all channels for an event type
  results in no notification of that type being sent to that master. \\
REQ-F-017 & The administrator authenticates with login and password; the
  system supports a single administrator account. & Only one administrator
  account exists and can log in. \\
REQ-F-018 & The administrator manages masters, services (with category),
  MasterService price links, and materials (with unit of measure). & Create
  / edit / deactivate operations are available for each of these reference
  entities. Deactivating a master, service, or material removes it from
  selection in new bookings or transactions but does not delete or hide it
  from existing appointment, payment, or transaction history (consistent
  with REQ-NF-005). \\
REQ-F-019 & The administrator can create, edit, and cancel client
  appointments. & An administrator-created or edited appointment is
  reflected identically to a client-made one (subject to REQ-F-002,
  REQ-F-003). \\
REQ-F-020 & The administrator records material receipt and issue
  transactions. & Each transaction updates the material's stock level by
  the recorded quantity. \\
REQ-F-021 & The administrator records payment (amount, status paid/unpaid,
  method) only after the service has been completed. & Payment cannot be
  recorded for an appointment whose service has not yet been marked
  completed. \\
REQ-F-022 & The administrator can view revenue reports for a fixed period
  (day/week/month) or a custom date range, a popular-services report, and
  current material stock levels. & Both a preset-period query and a custom
  from/to date range return a revenue total for that window. \\
REQ-F-023 & The administrator receives a notification (per their own
  preferences) for every appointment created, rescheduled, or cancelled,
  across all masters. & An event for any master's appointment produces a
  notification to the administrator unless disabled. \\
REQ-F-024 & The administrator creates services with a category of their own
  choosing; the system provides seed example categories (Hair, Coloring,
  Barbering) but does not restrict categories to a fixed list. & A new
  service can be saved with a category value not present in the seed
  examples. \\
REQ-F-025 & The administrator selects a material's unit of measure from the
  standard list (ml, l, g, kg, pcs) or adds a custom unit. & A material can
  be saved with a unit outside the standard list. \\
REQ-F-026 & A master or administrator can independently enable or disable
  each notification channel (SMS, in-app) for each event type (new,
  reschedule, cancellation). & Toggling one channel/event combination does
  not change any other channel/event combination's setting. \\
REQ-F-027 & The administrator marks an appointment as completed once the
  service(s) have been performed; this is a precondition for payment
  (REQ-F-021). \emph{(Added in v1.1, closes review finding A1.)} & An
  appointment cannot have a payment recorded (REQ-F-021) until it has been
  marked completed; after marking, its status is \texttt{completed}. \\
REQ-F-028 & When the administrator changes an appointment's date, time, or
  master (REQ-F-019), the system applies the same reminder recalculation
  (REQ-F-008) and sends the client the same updated-details SMS
  (REQ-F-009) as for a client-initiated reschedule. \emph{(Added in v1.1,
  closes review finding A5.)} & After an administrator changes an
  appointment's time, the stored 24h/2h reminder times match the new time,
  and one SMS with the updated details is recorded for the client. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

  a 30-minute boundary. \\

  with any online payment gateway. \emph{AC:} no payment-provider redirect
  or API call exists in the booking or payment flow. \\

\section{External Interface Requirements}

\subsection{User Interface}
Client-facing interface: a responsive website (REQ-NF-002), English
(REQ-NF-003), no login. Master and Administrator interfaces: a web
dashboard behind login (REQ-F-012, REQ-F-017).

\subsection{Communication Interfaces}
SMS delivery is simulated for this version (Section~\ref{sec:general}); no
real telecom gateway is integrated. In-app notifications (REQ-F-016,
REQ-F-023, REQ-F-026) require no external interface.

\section{BDD Scenarios}
\label{sec:bdd}

The following Gherkin-style scenarios cover the primary flows and the
significant edge cases identified during the SKED dialogue. Each scenario
lists the requirement IDs it exercises.

\begin{lstlisting}
# SCN-01 -- Successful multi-service booking
# Covers: REQ-F-001, REQ-F-002, REQ-F-003, REQ-F-004, REQ-F-005
Feature: Client books multiple services in one visit
  Scenario: Booking two services with a single available master
    Given a master "Olena" offers "Haircut" (30 min) and "Blow-dry" (30 min)
    And "Olena" has an open 60-minute slot on 2026-10-01 at 14:00
    When a client books "Haircut" and "Blow-dry" with "Olena" at that slot
    Then the appointment is confirmed immediately
    And the appointment duration is 60 minutes
    And the appointment cost is the sum of both service prices
    And a confirmation page is shown with cancel and reschedule links
    And exactly one confirmation SMS is recorded for the client

# SCN-02 -- Free cancellation with no restriction
# Covers: REQ-F-006
Feature: Client cancels an appointment
  Scenario: Cancelling minutes before the appointment
    Given a confirmed appointment starting in 10 minutes
    When the client opens the cancellation link and confirms cancellation
    Then the appointment status becomes "cancelled"
    And no cancellation fee is applied

# SCN-03 -- Free reschedule with reminder recalculation
# Covers: REQ-F-007, REQ-F-008, REQ-F-009
Feature: Client reschedules an appointment
  Scenario: Rescheduling to a new available slot
    Given a confirmed appointment on 2026-10-01 at 14:00
    And a new open slot with the same master on 2026-10-03 at 10:00
    When the client reschedules to the new slot via the link
    Then the appointment time becomes 2026-10-03 10:00
    And the 24-hour reminder is scheduled for 2026-10-02 10:00
    And the 2-hour reminder is scheduled for 2026-10-03 08:00
    And exactly one reschedule SMS is recorded for the client

# SCN-04 -- 24-hour reminder skipped for a late booking
# Covers: REQ-F-010
Feature: Reminder suppression for late bookings
  Scenario: Booking made less than 24 hours in advance
    Given the current time is 2026-10-01 10:00
    When a client books an appointment for 2026-10-02 08:00
    Then the 24-hour reminder is recorded with status "skipped"
    And the 2-hour reminder is scheduled normally for 2026-10-02 06:00

# SCN-05 -- Both reminders skipped for a very late booking
# Covers: REQ-F-010, REQ-F-011
Feature: Reminder suppression for very late bookings
  Scenario: Booking made less than 2 hours in advance
    Given the current time is 2026-10-01 10:00
    When a client books an appointment for 2026-10-01 11:00
    Then the 24-hour reminder is recorded with status "skipped"
    And the 2-hour reminder is recorded with status "skipped"

# SCN-06 -- Master disables SMS but keeps in-app notifications
# Covers: REQ-F-016, REQ-F-026
Feature: Per-channel notification preferences
  Scenario: Master receives only in-app notification for a new booking
    Given master "Olena" has disabled SMS for "new appointment"
    And master "Olena" has enabled in-app for "new appointment"
    When a client books an appointment with "Olena"
    Then "Olena" receives an in-app notification
    And "Olena" receives no SMS notification

# SCN-07 -- Payment recorded only after service completion
# Covers: REQ-F-021
Feature: Payment recording
  Scenario: Administrator attempts to record payment before completion
    Given an appointment that has not been marked as completed
    When the administrator attempts to record a payment for it
    Then the system rejects the payment recording

  Scenario: Administrator records payment after completion
    Given an appointment that has been marked as completed
    When the administrator records a payment of 800 UAH, status "paid", method "cash"
    Then the payment is saved and linked to that appointment

# SCN-08 -- Administrator creates a service with a custom category
# Covers: REQ-F-024
Feature: Free-form service categories
  Scenario: Creating a service outside the seed categories
    Given the seed categories "Hair", "Coloring", "Barbering" exist
    When the administrator creates a service "Eyebrow shaping" with category "Cosmetology"
    Then the service is saved with category "Cosmetology"
    And "Cosmetology" becomes selectable for future services
\end{lstlisting}
