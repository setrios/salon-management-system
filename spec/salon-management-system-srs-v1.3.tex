\noindent
\textbf{New assumptions introduced while closing these findings}
(not previously confirmed via the SKED dialogue --- require review before
this version is approved):

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2cm]

\item
REQ-F-027 assigns the ``mark appointment completed'' action to the
Administrator, matching the existing allocation of payment recording,
rather than to the Master.

\item
REQ-F-028 makes administrator-initiated date/time/master changes follow
the same reminder-recalculation and client-SMS rules as a client-initiated
reschedule, rather than being a silent edit.

\item
The confirmation page and its access token remain valid (read-only) after
cancellation, rather than being invalidated.

\item
REQ-F-029 (v1.2): a reminder previously recorded as skipped can become
active again if a reschedule moves it back into the future, rather than
staying permanently skipped.

\item
REQ-F-030 (v1.2): only the acting administrator is exempted from
notification of their own action; the assigned master is still notified.
(Masters cannot currently trigger these events themselves, so no
equivalent master-side exemption was needed.)

\item
REQ-F-031 (v1.2): multi-service booking duration is rounded up to the next
30-minute boundary for slot-occupancy purposes, rather than occupying an
exact, unaligned duration.

\item
REQ-F-032 (v1.3): the popular-services report ranks services by number of
bookings, not by revenue generated.

\item
REQ-F-032 (v1.3): the popular-services report accepts the same period
options as REQ-F-022 (preset or custom range), while REQ-F-033
(stock levels) accepts none, being a point-in-time snapshot.

\end{itemize}

\section{Introduction}

\subsection{Purpose}

This document specifies the baseline requirements for the Salon Management
System: an information system for client record-keeping and service booking
for a single-location unisex beauty salon. It supersedes the informal
concept note \texttt{DESCRIPTION.md} as the controlling requirements
artifact once approved at SPEC-GATE (Section~\ref{sec:gate}).

\subsection{Scope}

The system automates client booking, master schedule management, material
stock tracking (general accounting only), and basic financial reporting for
one salon location. It does not include a client account system, a loyalty
program, automatic payroll calculation, no-show tracking, recipe-based
material deduction, or an online payment gateway
(Section~\ref{sec:constraints}).

\subsection{Actors}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2cm]

\item
\textbf{Client} --- books a service without registration, using name and
phone number.

\item
\textbf{Master} --- performs services; manages their own schedule and sees
their own appointment calendar; authenticates with login/password.

\item
\textbf{Administrator} --- manages reference data, appointments,
materials, and reporting; single administrator account; authenticates
with login/password.

\end{itemize}

\subsection{Document Overview}

\section{General Description}
\label{sec:general}

\subsection{Superseded Decisions}
\label{sec:superseded}

Two decisions confirmed in \texttt{DESCRIPTION.md} \S8 were explicitly
revised by this SRS, as a result of the SKED dialogue.
(\texttt{DESCRIPTION.md} has since been synchronized with these revisions
via a separate \texttt{docs:} commit; this section documents the change
for historical traceability rather than describing a currently-open
conflict.)

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.2cm]

\item
\emph{``The only SMS notification sent is a reminder before the visit ---
there is no SMS for booking confirmation or cancellation.''}

\textbf{Revised:}
the system now sends a booking confirmation SMS and a reschedule
confirmation SMS (REQ-F-005, REQ-F-009). No additional SMS is sent
specifically for cancellation --- the client sees the updated status on
the confirmation page (REQ-F-004, REQ-F-006).

\item
\emph{``The master/administrator do not receive a notification when a new
booking is made or cancelled.''}

\textbf{Revised:}
masters and administrators now receive notifications for new, rescheduled,
and cancelled appointments, through independently configurable channels
(REQ-F-016, REQ-F-023, REQ-F-026).

\end{enumerate}

All other decisions in \texttt{DESCRIPTION.md} \S8 remain in force and are
treated as constraints of this SRS (Section~\ref{sec:constraints}).

\subsection{Product Functions}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2cm]

\item
Client: multi-service booking, immediate confirmation, free cancellation
and reschedule via a link, SMS notifications.

\item
Master: schedule view, own service/price list, availability management,
notifications.

\item
Administrator: reference data management, appointment management,
material tracking, payment recording, reporting, notifications.

\end{itemize}

\subsection{Constraints}
\label{sec:constraints}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2cm]

\item Single salon location, single time zone, no branch network.

\item 30-minute appointment slot granularity.

\item No client account/password; client identified by name and phone number
only.

\item No online payment gateway; only the fact of payment is recorded,
after service completion.

\item No automatic material deduction by service ``recipe''; materials
tracked as general receipt/issue accounting only.

\item No loyalty program, no automatic master payroll calculation, no
client no-show tracking.

\item No self-service password reset or account recovery for Master or
Administrator accounts; credential resets are an operational matter
outside this version's scope (added in v1.1, closing review finding A4).

\end{itemize}

\subsection{Assumptions and Dependencies}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2cm]

\item
SMS sending is simulated (no real SMS gateway integration in this
version), consistent with \texttt{DESCRIPTION.md}.

\item
Expected usage scale is not numerically fixed for this version; no
specific throughput or concurrency target is set as an NFR.

\end{itemize}

\section{Domain Glossary}
\label{sec:glossary}

\begin{longtable}{L{3.4cm}L{10.6cm}}
\toprule
Term & Definition \\
\midrule
\endhead

Client &
A person who books a service by providing name and phone number; has no
account in the system. \\

Master &
A staff member who performs services; authenticates with an individual
login (username and password) created by the administrator; has own
service/price list and own availability schedule. Self-service password
reset is out of scope (see Constraints). \\

Administrator &
The single staff account with full management access to reference data,
appointments, materials, and reports; authenticates with an individual
login (username and password). Self-service password reset is out of scope
(see Constraints). \\

Service &
A definition of a performable procedure: name, category, base duration.
Categories are free-form, created by the administrator
(see REQ-F-024); not a fixed enumeration. \\

MasterService &
The link between a master and a service, carrying that master's
individual price for that service. \\

Appointment &
A single client visit: client, master, date/time, status, and an
unguessable access token used for the confirmation page. Status is one of
\texttt{confirmed} (set automatically on creation, REQ-F-003),
\texttt{completed} (set by the administrator, REQ-F-027), or
\texttt{cancelled} (set by the client, REQ-F-006). A reschedule
(REQ-F-007, REQ-F-028) updates date/time and master on this same record
and does not create a new Appointment. \\

AppointmentService &
One of the services included in a single appointment; an appointment may
include several. \\

Confirmation Page &
The web page shown to the client immediately after booking and reachable
again via the link sent by SMS; shows appointment details and the
cancel/reschedule controls. The page and its access token remain valid
after cancellation: the page then shows status \texttt{cancelled} and
the cancel/reschedule controls are disabled rather than the link being
invalidated. \\

Payment &
A record of amount, status (paid/unpaid), and payment method for an
appointment; created only after the service is completed. \\

Material &
A stock item: name, unit of measure (from the standard list or a custom
unit), current stock level. \\

MaterialTransaction &
A recorded receipt or issue of a material: date, quantity. \\

Notification &
An event-driven message (booking confirmation, reschedule confirmation,
24-hour reminder, 2-hour reminder to the client; new/reschedule/
cancellation alert to master and administrator), each with a channel and
a send status (sent/skipped/failed). \\

Reminder &
The 24-hour or 2-hour client SMS before the appointment; skipped (not sent
retroactively) if its scheduled time has already passed at the moment of
booking or reschedule. \\

Notification Preference &
A master's or administrator's own on/off setting for a given notification
channel (SMS, in-app) and a given event type (new, reschedule,
cancellation). \\

\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\section{Functional Requirements}
\label{sec:functional}

Each requirement has a stable identifier \texttt{REQ-F-NNN}. Acceptance
criteria are stated as observable conditions.

\begin{longtable}{L{1.9cm}L{6.4cm}L{6.2cm}}
\toprule
ID & Requirement & Acceptance Criterion \\
\midrule
\endhead

REQ-F-001 &
The client can select one or more services for a single visit; total
duration and cost are the sum of the selected services. &
Booking with 2+ services shows a single duration and a single cost equal
to the sum of the individual services. \\

REQ-F-002 &
The client selects a master, date, and time from that master's available
30-minute slots. &
Only slots within the selected master's set availability, and not already
booked, are selectable. \\

REQ-F-003 &
The appointment is confirmed automatically on creation; no manual
approval step exists. &
Immediately after booking, the appointment status is ``confirmed'' with
no pending/approval state observable. \\

REQ-F-004 &
After a successful booking, the system shows a confirmation page with
appointment details and cancel/reschedule links. &
The page is displayed right after booking and is reachable again later
via the SMS link (REQ-F-005). \\

REQ-F-005 &
The system sends the client a booking confirmation SMS (simulated) with a
link to the confirmation page. &
Exactly one confirmation SMS per successful booking is recorded,
containing a valid link to REQ-F-004's page. \\

REQ-F-006 &
The client can cancel the appointment via the link, free of charge, with
no restriction on notice period. &
Cancelling at any time, including minutes before the appointment, succeeds
without a fee or error. \\

REQ-F-007 &
The client can reschedule the appointment via the link, free of charge,
with no restriction on notice period or number of times. &
Rescheduling repeatedly, including shortly before the appointment,
succeeds each time without a fee or error. \\

REQ-F-008 &
On reschedule, the 24-hour and 2-hour reminder times are recalculated from
the new appointment time. &
After a reschedule, the stored reminder times equal
new\_time $-$ 24h and new\_time $-$ 2h. \\

REQ-F-009 &
On reschedule, the system sends a new SMS with the updated appointment
details and a link to the (same) confirmation page. &
Exactly one reschedule SMS per successful reschedule is recorded. \\

REQ-F-010 &
The system sends a 24-hour reminder SMS, unless that time has already
passed at the moment of booking or reschedule, in which case it is
recorded as skipped. &
A booking made $<$24h before the appointment shows the 24h reminder with
status ``skipped''; otherwise ``sent'' at the correct time. \\

REQ-F-011 &
The system sends a 2-hour reminder SMS under the same rule as REQ-F-010. &
A booking made $<$2h before the appointment shows the 2h reminder with
status ``skipped''. \\

REQ-F-012 &
A master authenticates with login and password; the account is created by
the administrator. &
A master cannot self-register; login succeeds only for an account created
by the administrator. \\

REQ-F-013 &
A master can view their own appointment calendar. &
The calendar shown to a logged-in master contains only that master's
appointments. \\

REQ-F-014 &
A master can view the services they perform and their own prices. &
The list shown matches exactly the MasterService records for that master. \\

REQ-F-015 &
A master sets their availability using a recurring weekly pattern,
specific dates, or both. &
Both a weekly-repeating slot and a one-off date-specific slot can be
created and take effect for booking. \\

REQ-F-016 &
A master receives a notification (SMS and/or in-app, per their own
preferences) when one of their own appointments is created, rescheduled,
or cancelled. &
Disabling all channels for an event type results in no notification of
that type being sent to that master. \\

REQ-F-017 &
The administrator authenticates with login and password; the system
supports a single administrator account. &
Only one administrator account exists and can log in. \\

REQ-F-018 &
The administrator manages masters, services (with category), MasterService
price links, and materials (with unit of measure). &
Create/edit/deactivate operations are available for each of these
reference entities. Deactivating a master, service, or material removes
it from selection in new bookings or transactions but does not delete or
hide it from existing appointment, payment, or transaction history
(consistent with REQ-NF-005). \\

REQ-F-019 &
The administrator can create, edit, and cancel client appointments. &
An administrator-created or edited appointment is reflected identically
to a client-made one (subject to REQ-F-002, REQ-F-003). \\

REQ-F-020 &
The administrator records material receipt and issue transactions. &
Each transaction updates the material's stock level by the recorded
quantity. \\

REQ-F-021 &
The administrator records payment (amount, status paid/unpaid, method)
only after the service has been completed. &
Payment cannot be recorded for an appointment whose service has not yet
been marked completed. \\

REQ-F-022 &
The administrator can view revenue reports for a fixed period
(day/week/month) or a custom date range. &
Both a preset-period query and a custom from/to date range return a
revenue total for that window. \\

REQ-F-023 &
The administrator receives a notification (per their own preferences)
for every appointment created, rescheduled, or cancelled, across all
masters. &
An event for any master's appointment produces a notification to the
administrator unless disabled. \\

REQ-F-024 &
The administrator creates services with a category of their own choosing;
the system provides seed example categories (Hair, Coloring, Barbering)
but does not restrict categories to a fixed list. &
A fresh installation offers Hair, Coloring, and Barbering as selectable
categories before any administrator action, and a new service can also be
saved with a category value not present among them. \\

REQ-F-025 &
The administrator selects a material's unit of measure from the standard
list (ml, l, g, kg, pcs) or adds a custom unit. &
A material can be saved with a unit outside the standard list. \\

REQ-F-026 &
A master or administrator can independently enable or disable each
notification channel (SMS, in-app) for each event type (new, reschedule,
cancellation). &
Toggling one channel/event combination does not change any other
channel/event combination's setting. \\

REQ-F-027 &
The administrator marks an appointment as completed once the service(s)
have been performed; this is a precondition for payment (REQ-F-021).
\emph{(Added in v1.1, closes review finding A1.)} &
An appointment cannot have a payment recorded (REQ-F-021) until it has
been marked completed; after marking, its status is \texttt{completed}. \\

REQ-F-028 &
When the administrator changes an appointment's date, time, or master
(REQ-F-019), the system applies the same reminder recalculation
(REQ-F-008) and sends the client the same updated-details SMS (REQ-F-009)
as for a client-initiated reschedule.
\emph{(Added in v1.1, closes review finding A5.)} &
After an administrator changes an appointment's time, the stored 24h/2h
reminder times match the new time, and one SMS with the updated details is
recorded for the client. \\

REQ-F-029 &
When a reschedule (REQ-F-007, REQ-F-008) moves a reminder's recalculated
time back into the future, that reminder is (re)scheduled even if it had
previously been recorded as skipped.
\emph{(Added in v1.2, closes review finding D1.)} &
A reminder previously recorded as skipped, after a reschedule whose new
time places it in the future, is recorded as scheduled and is sent
normally at that new time. \\

REQ-F-030 &
When an appointment event (new, reschedule, cancellation) is caused by the
administrator's own action (REQ-F-019, REQ-F-028), the administrator does
not receive a notification for that specific event; the assigned master's
notification (REQ-F-016) is unaffected.
\emph{(Added in v1.2, closes review finding D2.)} &
An administrator who creates, edits, or cancels an appointment receives no
notification for that action, while the appointment's assigned master
still receives their own configured notification for the same event. \\

REQ-F-031 &
For slot-occupancy purposes, the total duration of a multi-service booking
(REQ-F-001) is rounded up to the next 30-minute boundary (REQ-NF-008).
\emph{(Added in v1.2, closes review finding D3.)} &
A booking with a summed service duration of 75 minutes occupies a
90-minute block of the master's availability, not an unaligned
75-minute block. \\

REQ-F-032 &
The administrator can view a popular-services report ranking services by
number of bookings, for the same period options as REQ-F-022 (a fixed
period or a custom date range).
\emph{(Added in v1.3; REQ-F-022 originally bundled this clause without a
matching acceptance criterion.)} &
A popular-services query for a given period returns services ordered by
descending booking count among appointments within that period. \\

REQ-F-033 &
The administrator can view current material stock levels as a point-in-time
snapshot; no period selection applies.
\emph{(Added in v1.3, same finding as REQ-F-032.)} &
A stock-levels query returns each material's current quantity, unaffected
by any period parameter. \\

\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\section{Non-Functional Requirements}
\label{sec:nonfunctional}

\begin{longtable}{L{1.9cm}L{2.6cm}L{9.9cm}}
\toprule
ID & Category & Requirement and Acceptance Criterion \\
\midrule
\endhead

REQ-NF-001 & Security &
Cancellation and reschedule links shall use a non-sequential,
non-guessable identifier (e.g., UUID) as the appointment access token.
\emph{AC:} incrementing or guessing a token value does not grant access
to another client's appointment. \\

REQ-NF-002 & Usability &
The client-facing booking web interface shall be usable on mobile
browsers via a responsive layout.
\emph{AC:} on a mobile-width viewport, the service/master/slot selection
form, the confirmation page, and the cancel/reschedule actions are each
fully usable without horizontal scrolling. \\

REQ-NF-003 & Localization &
The user interface language shall be English.
\emph{AC:} no interface text, label, or message is displayed in another
language. \\

REQ-NF-004 & Localization &
Monetary amounts shall be displayed in Ukrainian hryvnia (UAH).
\emph{AC:} all price and payment fields render with a UAH indicator. \\

REQ-NF-005 & Data retention &
Appointment and payment history shall be retained indefinitely; no
automatic deletion or archiving is implemented.
\emph{AC:} a record created in this version is still retrievable after an
arbitrary elapsed time, with no purge job present. \\

REQ-NF-006 & Privacy &
Client personal data (name, phone number) shall be handled under standard
data-hygiene practice; no additional regulatory compliance mechanism is
required for this version.
\emph{AC:} a client's name and phone number are visible only to an
authenticated master viewing their own appointments (REQ-F-013), the
administrator, and the client themself via their own confirmation-page
token (REQ-NF-001); the system transmits this data to no third-party
service (there is no payment gateway or other external integration,
REQ-NF-009). \\

REQ-NF-007 & Constraint (carried over) &
The system shall operate for a single salon location in a single time
zone.
\emph{AC:} no location-selection or time-zone-selection control exists
in the interface. \\

REQ-NF-008 & Constraint (carried over) &
All appointment scheduling shall use 30-minute time slots.
\emph{AC:} no bookable start time falls outside a 30-minute boundary. \\

REQ-NF-009 & Constraint (carried over) &
The system shall not integrate with any online payment gateway.
\emph{AC:} no payment-provider redirect or API call exists in the booking
or payment flow. \\

\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\section{External Interface Requirements}

\subsection{User Interface}

Client-facing interface: a responsive website (REQ-NF-002), English
(REQ-NF-003), no login. Master and Administrator interfaces: a web
dashboard behind login (REQ-F-012, REQ-F-017).

\subsection{Communication Interfaces}

SMS delivery is simulated for this version (Section~\ref{sec:general});
no real telecom gateway is integrated. In-app notifications
(REQ-F-016, REQ-F-023, REQ-F-026) require no external interface.

\section{BDD Scenarios}
\label{sec:bdd}

The following Gherkin-style scenarios cover the primary flows and the
significant edge cases identified during the SKED dialogue. Each scenario
lists the requirement IDs it exercises.

\begin{lstlisting}
# SCN-01 -- Successful multi-service booking
# Covers: REQ-F-001, REQ-F-002, REQ-F-003, REQ-F-004, REQ-F-005
Feature: Client books multiple services in one visit

Scenario: Booking two services with a single available master
Given a master "Olena" offers "Haircut" (30 min) and "Blow-dry" (30 min)
And "Olena" has an open 60-minute slot on 2026-10-01 at 14:00
When a client books "Haircut" and "Blow-dry" with "Olena" at that slot
Then the appointment is confirmed immediately
And the appointment duration is 60 minutes
And the appointment cost is the sum of both service prices
And a confirmation page is shown with cancel and reschedule links
And exactly one confirmation SMS is recorded for the client

# SCN-02 -- Free cancellation with no restriction
# Covers: REQ-F-006
Feature: Client cancels an appointment

Scenario: Cancelling minutes before the appointment
Given a confirmed appointment starting in 10 minutes
When the client opens the cancellation link and confirms cancellation
Then the appointment status becomes "cancelled"
And no cancellation fee is applied

# SCN-03 -- Free reschedule with reminder recalculation
# Covers: REQ-F-007, REQ-F-008, REQ-F-009
Feature: Client reschedules an appointment

Scenario: Rescheduling to a new available slot
Given a confirmed appointment on 2026-10-01 at 14:00
And a new open slot with the same master on 2026-10-03 at 10:00
When the client reschedules to the new slot via the link
Then the appointment time becomes 2026-10-03 10:00
And the 24-hour reminder is scheduled for 2026-10-02 10:00
And the 2-hour reminder is scheduled for 2026-10-03 08:00
And exactly one reschedule SMS is recorded for the client

# SCN-04 -- 24-hour reminder skipped for a late booking
# Covers: REQ-F-010
Feature: Reminder suppression for late bookings

Scenario: Booking made less than 24 hours in advance
Given the current time is 2026-10-01 10:00
When a client books an appointment for 2026-10-02 08:00
