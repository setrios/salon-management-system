**Scope decision (human, 2026-11-08).** `docs/ticket-list.md` describes SMS-14 as
"custom service category + seeds (SCN-08)". The human extended it: if S1.2
started from SMS-14 as titled, SMS-23 would be authorised to implement seventeen
operations with failing tests only for categories and seeds. The tests therefore
cover the whole surface SMS-23 implements — every operation gets at least one
status-and-shape assertion, plus a negative wherever the contract declares
401/403/404/409/422; SCN-08 stays as a scenario; the three seeds are asserted
present and immutable. This is an extension, not a replacement of SCN-08.

**Outside this gate.** The six public operations P1–P6 (`GET /services`,
`/services/{serviceId}`, `/categories`, `/masters`, `/masters/{masterId}`,
`/masters/{masterId}/services`) are owned by **S2.1**, the client booking flow
that needs them. They are outside R1–R17 and outside this gate's authorised
surface; they keep answering 404 until S2.1 mounts them. P3 in particular means
SCN-08's category list is observed through the administrator path (R9) at this
stage.

**Explicitly not tested** — the list the human signs, in §4. Nothing on that
list is a silent omission: each row states why no assertion exists.

## 6. Declared schema — `categories` (fixture)

The seed fixture writes this table before SMS-23 creates it, so its columns are
declared here, as plan §5.1 did for `masters` in S1.1.

| Column | Type | Constraints | Contract or decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| `id` | `uuid` | primary key, default `gen_random_uuid()` | row identity; not exposed by `Category` |
| `name` | `text` | not null, **unique** | `Category.name`; the fixture's `on conflict (name)` requires the unique constraint |
| `seeded` | `boolean` | not null, default `false` | `Category.seeded`; `true` exactly for the three seeds |
| `created_at` | `timestamptz` | not null, default `now()` | housekeeping, as on every other table |

The fixture writes only `name` and `seeded`. The rest is left to defaults. The
implementation must also make the category of a saved service appear with
`seeded:false`, and must never remove or flip the three seeds.
