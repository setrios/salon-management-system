## 5. What this gate cannot show

- **That the system is valid.** Validation (plan §10) is one signed record after
  the addendum; G4 is verification of the implementation against the contract and
  the SRS.
- **That the interface meets REQ-NF-002…004.** Those are interface properties;
  SMS-36 owns them and S5.5's gate says so. This work builds no UI.
- **That the reports are fast on a large catalogue.** No NFR states a target, and
  no operation in the contract is paginated for reports; the gate cannot invent a
  target to test against.
- **That concurrent load is safe beyond what the constraints enforce.** The
  unique constraints and the row locks are the backstop (`payments.appointment_id`,
  the appointment slot index, `material_transactions` under `FOR UPDATE`), but no
  load test exists and none is asked for.
- **That the log analysis of plan §5 was performed on production-like traffic.**
  The control runs trim the log to their own records; they show the shape of the
  records, not a lasting sample (SMS-47 owns log samples).
