## 1. What this gate decides

Whether stages S3 and S4 are finished. Four stages land here:

| Stage | Ticket | What it delivered |
|---|---|---|
| S3.1 | SMS-28 | P11 client reschedule with reminder recalculation and the reschedule row; the shared past-`startAt` rule for P8 and P11 (CL-11) |
| S3.2 | SMS-29 | the reminder engine: the 24 h and 2 h rows, the skip rules, reactivation on reschedule, and the dispatch on read |
| S4.1 | SMS-30 | channel × event preferences (M5, M6), the caller's own notification rows (M7), and the acting-administrator suppression |
| S4.2 | SMS-31 | the master's own calendar (M1) and own services (M2) |

The gate does **not** decide whether the system is valid. Plan §10 places
validation in one signed record after the addendum; G3 is verification, and §5
states what it therefore cannot show.

## 2. Criteria

Plan §8.3 gives six:

1. the whole suite is green, over the whole suite;
2. every ticket has a signed TEST-GATE and a `feat:` commit that follows it;
3. every stage has a control run in `logs/runs/`, the last of them against the
   built artifact;
4. `spec/api/api-traceability.md` names a Test and a Code location for each
   operation the stage delivered;
5. `npm run check:test-first` exits 0;
6. nothing is carried forward without a gate line naming the item, its owner and
   the stage it moves to.

## 5. What this gate cannot show

**(a) Validation is step 7, not G3.** Plan §10 places it in one signed record.
G3 is verification: the six criteria above. Closing SMS-6 closes two stages, not
the system.

**(b) The addendum is not selected here.** D-13 and §8.1 place the choice after
the base implementation is stable and before verification. Register row 10 is
the candidate; G3 does not choose it.

**(c) Ticket closures, and what each ticket's scope actually covers.**

| Ticket | Type | Closes at G3 | Basis, and the part another ticket covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| SMS-28 | Feature (S3.1) | yes | `reschedule.test.ts` R1–R20; gate `4e5581f`; feat `0b004e9`; `runs-08`. **Declares REQ-F-007…009; the reschedule SMS is the `reschedule` row of SMS-30's table** |
| SMS-29 | Feature (S3.2) | yes | `reminders.test.ts`; gate `543b5a7`; feat `372c992`; `runs-09`. **REQ-F-029's reactivation is asserted there; the delivery is the dispatch of row 11's caveat** |
| SMS-30 | Feature (S4.1) | yes | `notification-preferences.test.ts` P1–P13, `my-notifications.test.ts` N1–N8; gate `a5be7fd`; feat `3ecd3f8`; `runs-10`. **Declares REQ-F-030, whose test is SMS-18 in S5.1 (register row 12)** |
| SMS-31 | Feature (S4.2) | yes | `master-calendar.test.ts` A1–A10, B1–B5; gate `62adeea`; feat `b163d7e`; `runs-11`. **Declares REQ-F-013/014; SMS-21 declared them first and its row maps them here — SMS-21 is `Done` for auth and reference data, and this is where its calendar AC is proven** |
| SMS-10, SMS-11, SMS-12, SMS-17 | Tests | yes | their files are `reschedule.test.ts`, `reminders.test.ts`, `notification-preferences.test.ts`; all four were marked `Done` at their `docs:` commits |

Closing SMS-28 does not drag SMS-29 or SMS-30: they are separate stages with
separate files and separate gates.

**(d) Green is a moment, not a guarantee.** Criterion 1 was run on 2026-11-13
against `salon_test`. There is no CI: nothing re-runs the suite when a later
commit lands. The stage's protection is the signature and these records.

**(e) The simulation is still a simulation.** `sent` is the hand-off E3
describes — a status written, not a message transmitted. REQ-F-010's "sends" is
satisfied at the level of the record, and row 11 is the part of "at the correct
time" this build does not do.
