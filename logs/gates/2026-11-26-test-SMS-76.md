## 1. The findings

| # | The developer's words | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| V1 | A9 Reports: "From and To cannot be chosen, a click does not activate the field; Period works" | `admin-reports.ts` disables From/To until **Period** is *Custom range*: a literal reading of K10 ("Period … Custom range, then From / To"). The rule of REQ-F-022 (one form at a time) holds, but a disabled date field that ignores a click reads as broken |
| V2 | M3: "Remove and Add are one above the other with no margin between them" | `.row` has no style at all, and nothing separates the last row from the region's **Add** |
| V3 | M3: "Save … blends with the other buttons, nothing marks it, so it is easy to miss" | **Add** and **Save** share the primary style; the one action that stores the rules looks like the actions that only edit the form |

None of them is a new function: V1 keeps REQ-F-022's rule, V2 and V3 are what
REQ-NF-002 calls "fully usable".
