## 5. What this gate cannot show

The six criteria are met. (a), (c) and (d) are the places where meeting them is
not the same as having the evidence; (b) is a statement of fact, placed here
because it belongs to SMS-4's prose rather than to the six criteria.

**(a) "Verification and validation passed" — the ticket's prose, not the
criteria.** SMS-4's description in `docs/issue-import.csv` asks for
"verification and validation passed". Plan §8.3 lists six criteria and
validation is not among them; plan §10 places validation in a single signed
record at step 7, `logs/validation/YYYY-MM-DD-human-validation.md`, of which
`logs/validation/` currently holds only `.gitkeep`. Verification is what this
record's six rows are.

**Resolution.** G1 is a verification gate. Validation, per plan §10, is one
signed record at step 7 in `logs/validation/`, and it concerns the system, not a
stage. G1 neither claims it nor substitutes for it. Closing SMS-4 closes stage
S1, not the system's validation.

**(b) Ticket states.** SMS-4 asks that SMS-22/23/24 be `Done` and SMS-21 `Done`;
the same holds for SMS-14, 15 and 44, the test and infrastructure tickets these
three stages rest on. All seven were closed in the tracker on 2026-11-09, and
`docs/ticket-list.md` and `docs/issue-import.csv` were updated to `Done` in the
same working tree as this record. SMS-4 itself is closed by this record, not
before it.

**(c) Green is a moment, not a guarantee.** Criterion 1 was run on 2026-11-09
15:06 against `salon_test`. There is no CI: nothing re-runs the suite when a
later commit lands. The stage's protection against that is the signature and
these records, not a pipeline.

**(d) The record itself is not yet in history.** The three documentation files
this draft referred to are committed: `2ce8a47` (`runs-04`, `tdd-SMS-15`) and
`c04ecfc` (`api-traceability` §6). No source file differs from `07519d9`. This
record is untracked until it is signed and committed as
`gate: sign G1 for stage S1`, so that the signature lands in history together
with the evidence it signs — as it did for the three TEST-GATEs.
