format. No contract or SRS change is needed.

`tsc --noEmit` also fails at this commit, for the same reason: the tests name
`AppConfig.publicBaseUrl`, which does not exist yet. That is the red state seen
by the compiler, not a second defect.

U5/U6 compare against `config.publicBaseUrl` rather than a literal, because
`tests/setup.ts` loads a developer's `.env`, which may set `PUBLIC_BASE_URL`
or `PORT`; U1–U4 fix the environment explicitly with `loadConfig(env)`.

## 3. What the tests do not assert

That the page answers at the URL. Serving C2 is SMS-36; the browser tests of
SMS-57 open `confirmationUrl` and expect the page.
